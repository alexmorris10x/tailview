Keep your Mac within reach.

Check the app you left running. Fix a line of code. Pick up where you stopped, from your iPhone or iPad.

TailView brings your Mac’s screen, keyboard, and sound with you over your own Tailscale network.

Get TailViewSee the setup

iOS 17 or later. macOS 14 or later. Tailscale required.

Your desktop.
Your connection.

TailView shows the whole Mac desktop. Tap, scroll, drag, type, and paste text into the app you’re using. Listen to Mac audio, or send your iPhone’s microphone back to a Mac app.

Microphone return needs the separately installed BlackHole 2ch audio driver. Viewing, control, and listening to Mac sound do not.

Pair once. Stay in control.

A temporary code pairs your device with your Mac. Saved devices can reconnect while the host is sharing. Remote control is a separate choice on the Mac. Pause Access disconnects them immediately; Revoke removes a device’s permission.

Open on both sides.

The iOS viewer and Mac host source are available under Apache 2.0. Inspect how the connection works, build it yourself, or choose the convenient App Store download.

Try it on your own setup.

Download the iOS app free and choose the 7-day Trial when you’re ready. You confirm the free trial through the App Store.

After 7 days, remote viewing, control, and audio stop until you choose the lifetime unlock. Your saved Macs and settings remain.

No automatic charge. No subscription. Nothing to cancel.

$9.99 once, after the trial

Planned US launch price. The App Store shows your local price before you buy.

  • One purchase unlocks the iPhone and iPad app for the purchasing Apple Account.
  • The Mac companion is free.
  • Restore Purchases brings back your unlock after reinstalling or on another device with that Apple Account.

Tailscale is a separate service with its own plans and terms. TailView has no account service or hosted remote desktop.
