A Mac. A mobile device. Your network.

Set up both ends before starting your trial, so you can spend the seven days using TailView.

Launch status: the Mac companion is available. The iPhone/iPad App Store release is being prepared. Check download availability.

1. Connect Tailscale

You need a Mac running macOS 14 or later and an iPhone or iPad running iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. The Mac must be awake, signed in, and running TailView to connect.

Install Tailscale on both devices and sign them into the same network, often called a tailnet. Turn the VPN connection on. TailView does not create a Tailscale account for you.

If you manage network access rules, allow your mobile device to reach the Mac on TCP port 47820. Do not forward that port from your router to the public internet. Tailscale’s plans and terms apply separately.

2. Install the free Mac companion

Download the Mac disk image from TailView’s GitHub Releases. Open it, drag TailView Mac to Applications, and open the app there. If macOS says the download is damaged or cannot verify its developer, stop and check the release page; do not disable Gatekeeper.

The app stays in the menu bar when you close its window. Choose Open TailView there to return to setup. Launch at Login is optional and off by default.

3. Allow screen sharing and control

Screen Recording / Screen & System Audio Recording
In macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security, allow TailView Mac to capture the screen. This is how your desktop and Mac sound reach your device. macOS may ask you to quit and reopen TailView.
Accessibility
In TailView Mac, turn on Allow paired devices to control this Mac, then allow the app under Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Both choices are needed to click, type, scroll, and move windows. Grant permission only to the copy in Applications. Viewing works with control turned off.

Keep unrelated sensitive windows closed when sharing. Pair only devices you trust: they can see the entire shared display and act as you in the apps open on the Mac.

4. Pair first, then start the trial

  1. On the Mac, choose Start sharing, or Resume Access if you previously paused it. Sharing is off on a fresh installation and remembers your choice afterward. Choose Pair a new device. Keep the displayed address and code private.
  2. Open TailView on your iPhone or iPad. Enter the Mac address and temporary code, then choose Pair Mac. Pairing does not start your trial. Codes expire after five minutes or one successful pairing.
  3. When you are ready, open Trial and lifetime access, review the terms, and choose Start 7-day free trial. Confirm the free 7-day Trial item in Apple's purchase sheet. You can also buy the lifetime unlock or restore an existing purchase.
  4. Connect to your saved Mac. If iOS asks for Local Network access, allow it. Microphone access is optional; allow it only when you want to send your voice to the Mac.

Saved devices reconnect without entering another code. Up to two devices can view and control the Mac at once. One device at a time handles audio; use the audio-device setting to switch ownership.

5. Use the controls

Tap to click, slide one finger to move the pointer, press and hold to drag, two-finger tap to right-click, and swipe two fingers to scroll. Pinch to zoom. Keyboard opens remote typing; Paste sends text from your device’s clipboard to the Mac’s clipboard and focused app.

Mic and Speaker control the two audio directions independently. Settings lets you choose automatic audio and lock-screen controls. Lock-screen controls can act on the Mac without unlocking the phone, so turn them off if that is not appropriate for your setup.

Optional: send your microphone to a Mac app

Install BlackHole 2ch separately on the Mac. TailView does not bundle it. Choose BlackHole 2ch as the input in a Mac app that has its own input selection. In TailView Mac, optionally turn on Automatically route audio for the remote microphone. With that setting enabled during a connected session, TailView uses BlackHole as the Mac’s default input and preserves a physical output for Mac sound. It restores the previous routes after the last viewer disconnects, access is paused, or TailView quits normally. With the setting off, TailView leaves your system default audio routes alone.

Screen sharing, remote control, and Mac sound playback work without BlackHole. The iOS microphone prompt can be declined while using those features.

Stop sharing or remove a device

Disconnect stops the current mobile session. Pause Access on the Mac disconnects every viewer and stops capture and remote input. Pause remains in effect after relaunch until you choose Resume Access. Under Paired devices, Revoke removes that device’s access.

Forget in the iOS app removes its saved Mac profile. Revoke it on the Mac too if you are retiring the device. Deleting an app alone may leave Keychain records behind.

Update or uninstall

Update the Mac host first, then the iOS app. Quit TailView Mac, download the new signed release, and replace the copy in Applications. Update the iOS app through the App Store. Saved settings and pairings normally remain; pair again if the host rejects an old credential.

To uninstall, restore any darkened or disconnected display, pause access, revoke paired devices, disable Launch at Login, and quit TailView Mac. Then remove the Mac app from Applications and delete the iOS app. Tailscale and BlackHole are separate installations; remove them using their own instructions only if you no longer need them.
