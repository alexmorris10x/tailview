Mac Remote for your Mac

The free companion that lets your iPhone or iPad see and control this Mac.

Download for Mac

Version 1.0.1, signed and notarized. Apple silicon and Intel. macOS 14 or later.

Then

  1. Open the download and drag Mac Remote to Applications.
  2. Open Mac Remote, choose Start sharing and allow Screen Recording.
  3. Choose Pair a new device and enter the address and code in Mac Remote on your iPhone or iPad.

Both devices need Tailscale, signed in to the same network. Full setup guide
